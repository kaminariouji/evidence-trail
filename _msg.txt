Add the bounty spotlight direction: a Multi-KOL narrative agent

The Autonomous Agents bounty names a spotlight project — watch a set of market
voices, extract what they say about a token, detect convergence, open a practice
position under user-defined risk limits. src/kol.mjs is that agent, built the same
way as the price rules: named rules, no black box, same posts always produce the
same signal and the same ledger head.

- parsePost: cashtags + configured token list, bull/bear lexicons, conviction from
  strong phrases and hedges, urgency from urgency phrases. A tie scores 0 and can
  never open a position.
- convergence: distinct KOLs inside a window, same token, same direction. The gate
  is two hard conditions — at least three distinct voices AND score >= 1.2 — so one
  loud voice cannot outvote three calm ones.
- sizePosition: risk per trade is a fraction of equity, the stop sets the distance,
  and the notional cap binds before risk does. It never raises risk to fill a cap.
- runKolCycle: observation -> decision -> position, appended to the same
  hash-chained ledger, with a sha256 of every post that fed the decision.

The fixture (12 posts, 8 voices) yields one signal: SOL, 4 voices, score 3.27, a
300 USDC paper long capped by maxNotionalUsd. ARB has one voice and is not traded;
ETH is only bearish and this agent does not short.

6 new tests (26 total), including determinism: two runs over the same posts must
produce the same ledger head. The posts are a fixture, not a live feed, and no
social credentials are used anywhere.
